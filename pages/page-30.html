הענף הב': ביאור אגרת רב שרירא גאון — Branch Two: Elucidation of Rav Sherira Gaon's Epistle
שערי תשובה סי' קפ"ז

צילום כתב היד / Scan

נוסח עברי

(של ראשוני ראשונים שהזכיר למעלה) החכמה דתנן וכו', ונסתמו מעינות החכמה (יוחסין) והכי הוה נמי ביומי דרבי" (וכשנפטר רבי (שעליו קאי הגאון למעלה סמוך ונראה, וכד אסתיים משנה ונח נפשיה דרבי) נולדו ספקות אחרות בכונת סידור משנתו, ודעתו ר"ע, אם מפני סידור משניותיו, אם מפני קיצור לשונו, ואם מפני שנגרר אחר דעת יחיד, וע"ז יצאו ואתוספו פלפולים חדשים וז"א:) "ותקינו בה דקדוקי אחריני מלתא דבתר מלתא למאי אתניא ואמרינן מכדי תנא ליה הא, למה לי למתני הא? והיכא דלא יכיל לתרוצה אמרינן ביבמות, כאן שנה רבי משנה שאינה צריכה, ומפקינן אצרכתא דכל מלתא ומלתא, ואמרינן מאי קמ"ל? ופרטין פרטי, ומדמיין דמויי, כולהו הני מילי, כד הוה בימי רבי, הוה פריש להו פירושא דכל מלתא ומלתא, למאי הלכתיה, ואצטריכו למילי אחרנייתא דלא הוו צריכין להון קמאי היכא דחזא רבי מדעם ולא חזיוה רבנן בתראי פרשהו כי היכי דלא לסמוך עליה כגון דסתם רבי דברי יחיד (יז) וכו', ורבות כאלה,וכו', ו"היכא דסתים' במקום מחלוקתוכו', ואמרינן בה חולץ וכו' וכו', ואי אית במתני' שבשתא, וברייתא עדיפא מינה, מברירנן לה למלתא וכו', ואי פליגי תנאי, אמרינן במאי קא מפלגי וכו' ואליבא דמאן נינהו" ע"כ האריך הגאון להראות בראיות מופתיות, שלבד מסיבת סידור המשניות, ודעתו של רבי בפסק הלכה נולדו עוד פלפולים אחרים, ונארך התלמוד הקדמון באנפי אחריני, וזה מלבד קושיות ובעיות, ודרשות רבנן בתראי, ולזה השלים דבריו באומרו: לבד מן פלוגתא דאמוראי וקושיהון, ושינויהון, ותיובתהון, וכו' ולמכתב ארחי (ח"מ, ויוחסין ד"ק ור"א "ארמי" (יח) טעות גמור) דתלמודא (א"א) דנפישי טובא" כלל דברי הגאון בכל האמור הוא להשמיענו שבין כל אופני לימודים שזכר מריש ועד כאן, ובין כל אותם שלא בא זכרם מפני ריבויים, הן כולם כאחד נקראים בכללם תלמוד, אלא שבאותם הדורות לא היו נקראים ונלמדים ונשמעים על אופן אחד, כמו שבאמת עד היום כן אנו עושים לתלמידינו לפרש ולכתוב הדברים, לפי כח הלמד והמלמד, ולזה סיים עוד: "והכי הוה בעידנא דרבנן קמאי וכו' וכו' לפום מאי דיכיל מנהון, עד לדמויי דמויי" ונתן טעם הגאון כאלו יאמר אל תתמה על החפץ, איך יצוייר זה? שהרי כבר אמרנו דקודם דאפטר רבי חבור כתוב ומסודר היה הענין והסדר הזה במשניות גופייהו וז"א: "והא אמרינן דעד דאפטר רבי, גמרהון דכולהו תלמידי" (שהוא חבור המשנה) כל חד וחד תלמיד לפני רבו הוה וקמי (כצ"ל) דאתריץ מתני' כל חד וחד תלמודו לפי משנתו היה" (ר"ל כל תלמיד הוה גריס מתניתיה כלשון רבו) ובתר דאתרצה (רבי לסדר המשנה) בכל יומי דרבי כל חד וחד (מהמלמדים) פשיט להון (לתלמידיו) מתניתין (בלשון שסדרה רבי, וכפי דעתו ר"ע ושוב מלמדם טעמי הלכתא וזהו אומרו:) "והוה גמר להו טעמי הלכתא דיליה (אבל בלשון שהיה יודע כמו שהיה קודם סידור המשניות) ובתר דנח נפשיה דרבי אצטריכו למלקטיה, ולמגרסיה בפה אחד ולשון אחד" (יט) ואחר שיסד הגאון היסוד האמתי

English Translation

The wisdom that we learned, etc., and the founts of wisdom were sealed. The book Yuchasin states: "And so it was in the days of Rebbi (Rabbi Judah the Prince)." When Rebbi, to whom the Gaon referred above, passed away, and it appears that when the Mishnah was concluded and Rebbi rested from life, other doubts arose concerning the intent of the arrangement of his Mishnah, and his opinion, Rabbi Akiva, whether on account of the arrangement of his Mishnaic statements, or on account of the brevity of his language, or because he was drawn after a singular opinion. And for this, new dialectical discussions emerged and were added, and this is what is stated: "And they established other precise examinations within it, from one matter to the next, as to why it was taught. And we say, 'Since this was taught, why must this other be taught?' And where it cannot be resolved, we say in Tractate Yevamot: 'Here Rebbi taught a Mishnah that is not necessary.' And we extract the necessity of every single matter, and we say, 'What does it come to teach us?' And they explicate details, and they draw analogies. All these matters, when it was in the days of Rebbi, he would explain to them the exposition of every single matter, for what Halakhic purpose, and they were needed for other matters which the earlier ones did not require. Where Rebbi saw something which the later Rabbis did not see, they explained it so as not to rely upon it, such as when he recorded words in an unspecified manner, and many such cases, etc. And 'where it is unspecified' in the place of a dispute, etc., and we say regarding it, 'he performs Chalitzah,' etc., etc. And if there is an error in the Mishnah, and a Baraita is superior to it, we clarify the matter, etc. And if Tannaim dispute, we say, 'Concerning what do they dispute?' etc., 'And according to whose opinion are they?'"
Up to this point, the Gaon lengthened his discourse to demonstrate with conclusive proofs that, apart from the reason for the arrangement of the Mishnayot and Rebbi's opinion in Halakhic rulings, other dialectical discussions arose, and the ancient Talmud was extended in other aspects. This is besides the questions, problems, and expositions of the later Rabbis. Therefore, he concluded his words by stating, "apart from the disputes of the Amoraim and their questions, their variations, their refutations, etc., and to write the paths of Chiddushei Maharitz, and Yuchasin, Diqdūqei Rabbi Akiva, and Rabbi Akiva 'Arami' (a complete error) concerning the Talmud, which are very numerous." The sum of the Gaon's words in all that has been stated is to make us hear that among all the modes of study he mentioned from the beginning until now, and among all those whose mention has been omitted due to their multitude, they are all collectively called Talmud; however, in those generations, they were not called, learned, or heard in a single manner, just as indeed to this day we do for our students, to explain and write the matters, according to the ability of the learner and the teacher. For this reason, he further concluded: "And so it was in the time of the early Rabbis, etc., etc., according to what they could of them, up to drawing analogies."
And the Gaon gave a reason as if to say, "Do not wonder at the multitude, how is this conceivable? For we have already said that before Rebbi passed away, this matter and this order existed as a written and arranged compilation within the Mishnayot themselves." And this is what is stated: "And indeed we say that until Rebbi passed away, the learning of all the disciples (which is the compilation of the Mishnah), each and every disciple was before his master, and it must be said that when the Mishnah was resolved, each and every one's study was according to his Mishnah (that is to say, every student would recite his Mishnah according to the wording of his master)." And after Rebbi resolved the arrangement of the Mishnah, throughout Rebbi's days, each and every one of the teachers explained to their students the Mishnah (in the language that Rebbi arranged it, and according to his own opinion), and again taught them the reasons for the Halakhah. And this is what he states: "And he would teach them the Halakhic reasons according to his own understanding (but in the language he knew, as it was before the arrangement of the Mishnayot). And after Rebbi rested from life, they were compelled to collect it and to study it with a single mouth and a single language."
And after the Gaon established this true foundation, I decree, by the power of love, to the discerning reader, that if he possesses a copy of Chiddushei Maharitz, he should forthwith, upon uttering these words, rise like a hero and take for me from there these matters which we truly stand upon, which I have copied here letter for letter from the Yuchasin. And it is clear to him that he will not understand even a single letter, and from the abundance of his conversation and his anger, he will roar like a lion from the groaning of his heart, and he will call out in a loud voice: "These mice of the shredders who consume the choicest of the choicest! Their lame, and rejected, and broken text is the version! And why should he not mercifully be put to death, and not see the light of print alive?" Here we must offer thanks to the lack of knowledge of the copyist, whether French or Ashkenazi, who in Chiddushei Maharitz wrote *Arachai* with a full Vav, and thereby the scribes who emptied from one writing to another did not err, unlike what the complete knowledge of the Sephardic copyist led us to believe, in the precise writing where he wrote *Orchai* as defective. And when the copyists emptied from one writing to another, the Chet appeared to them as a Mem, and thus they produced "Arami" instead of *Orchai*, which they could not have mistaken in the accurate version written either as *Orchai* or *Orchai* with a full Vav. Meaning, that they were compelled to gather the Talmud, which is the explanation of the reasons for the Mishnah, and to arrange it in a single, ordered language, so that they could study it by heart, and also this.
