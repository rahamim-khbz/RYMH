English Translation

Section 187: Rabbenu Sherira, of blessed memory.
Regarding your report that there are students among you—"little foxes" lacking any real substance (*Sha'arei Tzedek*)— who dissent and question the *Geonim*, those pillars of the world, asking: "From whence did they derive this matter?"
